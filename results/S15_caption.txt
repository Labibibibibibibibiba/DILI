**Figure 2.** Robustness of the risk ranking to modelling choices. Results are from an independent re-implementation of the pipeline on a fixed 444/112 stratified split (Methods 2.9). **A.** Five-fold cross-validated AUROC under the four label-encoding schemes (L1 Most vs No, n = 556; L2 expanded-positive (Most + Less) vs No, n = 884; L3 ordinal three-class, n = 884; L4 four-class folded, n = 556); the primary two-class scheme (L1) and the four-class folded scheme (L4) perform comparably, whereas the expanded-positive (L2) and ordinal (L3) schemes are weaker. **B.** Five-fold cross-validated AUROC of the five descriptor spaces; the Morgan fingerprint alone (F1) and its concatenation with the physicochemical descriptors (F3) are the strongest, the physicochemical descriptors alone (F2) are markedly weaker, and the 3D-containing spaces (F4, F5; hatched) give no gain over 2D — adding the 3D descriptors to the 2D space (F5 vs F3) gives ΔAUROC = −0.015 [−0.024, −0.005], failing the pre-registered threshold of ΔAUROC ≥ 0.03 with an interval excluding zero. **C.** Consensus layering of the 659 uncategorised compounds by how many of the seven scoring configurations place them in the high-score tail (top 10%): **(C1)** layer size (11 high-consensus, 205 discordant, 443 low-consensus) and **(C2)** the distribution of the vote count $n_{high}$, which shows that no compound is placed in the tail by all seven models. **D.** Rank range of the prespecified validation panel across all 36 combinations of label-encoding (4) × classifier (3) × descriptor space (3) of the 659-compound ranking; the high-risk candidates stay high and the negative controls stay low, and the widest intervals (up to rank 598 for Cabotegravir) arise from the alternative encodings and the weak physicochemical space rather than from the primary configuration. All panels are derived from an independent re-implementation using a fixed 444/112 stratified split; the rank intervals in **D** are therefore not numerically comparable with the primary risk scores of Section 3.5.
